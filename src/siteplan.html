Site Plan Document

1. Site Purpose

The STOREKEEP application is a web, modular dashboard-style homepage designed to help e-commerce store owners manage their shop and anticipate shipping problems.

Often, store owners send out packages without knowing if bad weather at the destination will cause delays. Using the Shopify Admin API and the Open-Meteo API, this project will allow store owners to view their orders, products, and customers, while automatically checking the weather forecast for where an order is going.

2. Scenarios & Use Cases

The following scenarios outline how target users interact with STOREKEEP to solve their daily fulfillment challenges:

  • Scenario 1: Proactive Customer Communication

    Need: A store owner wants to reduce support tickets from angry customers complaining about late deliveries due to winter storms.

    Solution: The owner checks the STOREKEEP dashboard, clicks on recent orders, and sees a severe snowstorm alert generated by the Open-Meteo API for an order headed to Denver. They proactively email the customer to warn them of a likely 2-day delay, resulting in a positive customer service experience.

  • Scenario 2: Quick Inventory Checks

    Need: A merchant needs to quickly verify if they have enough stock of a popular item before running a flash sale on social media.

    Solution: The merchant opens the app on their phone. The application fetches live data from the Shopify Admin API, displaying the "Inventory Stock Summary" widget instantly. They confirm they have 50 units left and proceed with the marketing campaign.

  • Scenario 3: Streamlining the Daily Shipping Workflow

    Need: A solo entrepreneur spends too much time switching between their Shopify admin panel and external weather websites to plan their shipping queue.

    Solution: STOREKEEP acts as a centralized hub. The owner views the "Sales/Order Summary" widget alongside the "Shipping and Weather Insight" widget. They prioritize shipping orders to clear-weather destinations first, leaving storm-affected orders for the following day, optimizing their fulfillment process entirely within one window.

  • Scenario 4: Easy Access on Mobile

    Need: A store owner is away from their computer but needs to check on the status of a specific, high-value order.

    Solution: Because STOREKEEP is built as a responsive Single-Page Application (SPA), the owner accesses the dashboard via their smartphone browser. The sidebar slides in cleanly, they navigate to "Orders," and check the status without needing to log into the heavy, complex Shopify mobile app.

4. Typography

Heading Font: Roboto (Sans-serif)
This font is used for all major headers, titles, and widget names. It provides a clean, structural look that fits a dashboard environment.
Paragraph Font: Inter (Sans-serif)
This font is used for all body text, paragraphs, and data readouts. Inter is highly legible at small sizes, which is crucial for reading data tables and customer information.
